# Decisions

<!-- GA-GENERATED:index -->

- [Account deletion ships with the Play app, in the app and from a public web page](account-deletion-for-play.md) - Delete account moves out of usability part 8 into the Play Store work, with Google re-confirmation by redirect; a public /delete-account page gives Play's web link, with the in-app steps and an email request for people who can't sign in.
- [Ask your tasks reads the tasks into the prompt, on its own screen, one question at a time](ai-ask-tasks-long-context.md) - The function puts up to 500 of the account's tasks into the prompt instead of using embeddings; questions are asked on a new Ask screen, one at a time, with no history kept.
- [Task assists run only when pressed, and a rewrite can be undone](ai-assists-on-request.md) - Suggest labels, Rewrite and Translate each spend a request only when the person presses them; a rewritten or translated description replaces the old one at once, with Undo in the snackbar.
- [Digests are opt-in, count against the monthly limit, and fall back to plain text](ai-digests-opt-in.md) - The morning plan and weekly review are off until the person turns them on; each AI digest is one of the 300 monthly requests, and when none are left or the AI fails a plain digest written by code goes instead; the AI sees at most 30 tasks' titles, dates, times and labels.
- [The AI features are built in seven parts, foundation first](ai-features-roadmap.md) - Ten paid AI features are grouped into seven parts — foundation with fill-from-text, task assists, capture, digests, overdue triage, ask your tasks, and payments last — each with its own spec and plan.
- [Each Pro account gets 300 AI requests a month](ai-monthly-limit.md) - The function counts each account's AI requests per calendar month and refuses any past the limit, because AI spend shares the budget whose kill-switch also stops reminders.
- [Overdue triage only proposes new dates, on request, and is applied from a review list](ai-overdue-triage.md) - "Sort out" on the overdue tasks asks the AI for a new date in the next 14 days or no date for each, never done or deleted; the person ticks and edits the proposals, and the moves are undone together.
- [A photo gives a list of proposed tasks to tick before any is added](ai-photo-review-list.md) - Photo to tasks answers with up to ten proposed tasks; the person ticks and edits them, and only the ticked ones are saved.
- [A photo travels inside its AI request, shrunk by the app, with no Storage](ai-photos-in-request.md) - The app shrinks a picked photo to a small JPEG and puts it in the aiRequests document, which the rules size-check and the function clears once it answers; Firebase Storage isn't used.
- [AI calls go through a provider layer that switches between Claude and Gemini](ai-provider-switchable.md) - Every AI feature calls one provider interface, so Claude and Gemini can be swapped without changing the features; it starts on Claude Haiku.
- [AI requests travel as Firestore documents, not callable functions](ai-requests-via-firestore.md) - The app writes an aiRequests document, a Firestore-triggered function calls the AI and writes the answer back, so no function answers HTTP or callable requests.
- [Suggested steps are added after the checklist's items, at most eight at a time](ai-steps-appended.md) - "Suggest steps" appends up to eight new checklist items after the existing ones, skipping any that repeat an item, never replacing what the person wrote, with Undo.
- [Voice capture uses the browser's speech recognition, feeding Fill from text](ai-voice-browser-speech.md) - Dictation types into the Fill from text box through the browser's own speech recognition, so no audio reaches the AI provider; the site's Permissions-Policy allows the microphone for itself.
- [The Android app is in.spanion.todo.pwa, generated with Bubblewrap](android-app-bubblewrap.md) - The Play Store app is a Trusted Web Activity with package in.spanion.todo.pwa, made and rebuilt with the Bubblewrap CLI, opening todo.spanion.in; the never-published PWABuilder project app.web.spanion_todo.twa is removed.
- [Calendar tasks move by drag or a Move button, and empty space adds one](calendar-move-and-add.md) - Todo tasks are dragged to another day or time on the calendar (15-minute steps in week and day view, press and hold on touch), with a Move button for the keyboard; clicking empty space opens the add screen with that date.
- [Continue the existing todo-pwa codebase](continue-existing-codebase.md) - The code in this repository is built on where it is; it isn't rewritten, migrated or treated as reference only.
- [Data exports as JSON, CSV and .ics, imports from JSON as new, and account deletion is finished by a function](data-export-import-and-account-deletion.md) - Profile exports tasks as JSON (complete), CSV and .ics; import accepts only the app's JSON and adds everything as new; deleting the account re-confirms the user, deletes the Auth user, and an auth-deletion trigger erases the data.
- [Facebook sign-in is removed](facebook-sign-in-removed.md) - Only email and password, and Google, remain; accounts that only ever used Facebook can no longer sign in.
- [Google sign-in uses a same-origin redirect everywhere, never a popup](google-sign-in-redirect-only.md) - Signing in, signing up and re-confirming before deleting the account all go to Google by redirect through the page's own /__/auth/handler; the popup path is removed because popups are unreliable in the Android TWA.
- [Home opens on a Today view with overdue tasks first](home-views.md) - Home gets Today, Upcoming, Overdue, No date and All views beside the date bar; overdue means dated before today, and undated tasks no longer show on every day.
- [Calendar export is an .ics file made in the browser](ics-export-client-side.md) - Tasks reach other calendars as an .ics file the app builds and downloads; there is no subscribable feed, so no function answers HTTP.
- [The iOS wrapper isn't shipped or maintained](ios-wrapper-unmaintained.md) - packages/ios stays in the repository as it is, but nothing ships it, and changes to the site needn't keep it working.
- [Labels stay create-only](labels-create-only.md) - Users create labels but don't rename, recolour or delete them; the app needs no edit or delete for labels.
- [Old tasks are taken to be in Asia/Kolkata](legacy-tasks-asia-kolkata.md) - Tasks the old app saved, which carry no time zone, are read and migrated as India time, the zone the old app and its reminder assumed.
- [The privacy policy and account-deletion pages are in English only](legal-pages-english-only.md) - /privacy and /delete-account are public pages written in English only, even in Hindi, because a translated legal text could say something the English doesn't.
- [The modernisation's gates pass](modernisation-verified.md) - The developer confirmed that typecheck, lint, format, unit, rules, functions and e2e tests and the build pass, so the fix report's "nothing has been run yet" warning is out of date.
- [The emulators run on their own ports](non-default-emulator-ports.md) - Auth, Firestore and Functions emulators use ports other than Firebase's defaults, set once in firebase.json, because another local project uses the defaults.
- [Pro is sold through Play Billing in the Android app and Razorpay on the web](payments-play-and-razorpay.md) - The Android app sells Pro only through Google Play Billing; the website sells it through Razorpay subscriptions; prices and plans are set in those providers' dashboards, not in code.
- [Payments reach the server without an HTTP function](payments-without-http.md) - The app hands each payment result to the function through a request document to verify; Play's renewals arrive by Pub/Sub, and Razorpay subscriptions are checked once a day, so the no-HTTP rule stands.
- [Pro is turned on by hand with an admin script until payments exist](pro-granted-by-admin-script.md) - Until the payments part is built, an account gets the paid AI features only when someone runs an admin script for it; no payment code comes before then.
- [Push reminders are kept, on the Blaze plan](push-reminders-on-blaze.md) - Reminders stay, which needs scheduled functions and so the pay-as-you-go Blaze plan, capped by the budget kill-switch.
- [Quick add reads dates with its own English and Hindi parser](quick-capture.md) - Quick add on Home reads a fixed set of date and time phrases in English and Hindi with a parser of the app's own; the web app gets icon shortcuts and a share target, the Android app later; keys reach cards.
- [A repeating task creates its next occurrence when it is done](recurring-tasks.md) - Each occurrence is an ordinary task; finishing one creates the next on the first pattern date after both its date and today, copying it with the checklist unticked; edits carry forward and done history stays.
- [Reminders go at a lead time each task chooses, with Mark done and Snooze that open the app](reminder-lead-time-and-actions.md) - Each task picks how long before it is due to be reminded (stored as remindBefore and notifyAt); the push carries Mark done and Snooze buttons that open the app to act; snooze is 15 minutes or 1 hour.
- [Saves never time out or retry](saves-never-time-out.md) - A save waits for Firestore's answer however long it takes, because a timeout or a retry risks writing twice; the form says it is still saving after 15 s.
- [New Pinia stores use the setup style](setup-style-stores.md) - A new store is written as a setup store, like the session store; the four options stores stay as they are until they are changed for another reason.
- [Tasks get a four-level priority that sorts within a day, and a checklist field](task-priority-and-checklist.md) - A task has a priority (none, low, medium, high) that orders tasks within a day before their time, and a checklist of up to 50 items stored in its own field.
- [@types/node follows the Node each package runs on](types-node-follows-runtime.md) - The app's @types/node stays on the major of its tooling's Node (.nvmrc) and the functions' on their runtime's (nodejs22); Dependabot ignores majors past them.
- [Deletes on the board are undone, not confirmed](undo-instead-of-confirm.md) - Deleting a task happens at once and offers Undo instead of asking first; Clear done still asks, deletes only the done tasks the filter shows, and offers Undo too.
- [Usability features are built in eight parts, in order](usability-roadmap.md) - Nineteen usability features are grouped into eight parts — board fixes, views, faster capture, calendar, task fields, reminders, recurring tasks, data and account — each with its own spec and plan.

# The blue double tick, everywhere — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: the project's workflow policy names the executor (`run.executor`): ga:implement-with-subagents by default, or ga:implement. Follow the policy and this plan's Checkpoints. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Show the blue double tick wherever the web app shows a logo: the browser tab, the loading screen and above the sign-in and sign-up cards.

**Architecture:** One Vue component, `AppLogo`, draws the tick as an inline SVG from three polygons traced from the 512 px icon; the two auth screens use it. `index.html` inlines the same polygons for the loading screen, since Vue isn't loaded yet. The favicon is redrawn from the 512 px icon, and the two unused logo files are deleted.

**Tech Stack:** Vue 3.5 with `<script setup lang="ts">`, Vuetify 4 (md2 blueprint), Vite 8, Vitest with `@vue/test-utils` in jsdom.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-11-05-app-logo.md`

## Global Constraints

- The tick is filled `#2196F3` in light and dark (spec L3).
- The logo is decorative wherever it is used: the SVG is `aria-hidden="true"` and has no title (spec L3).
- The SVG viewBox is `0 104 512 308` (spec L3).
- The menu and the app bar get no logo (spec, "Not changing").
- Native Android and iOS icons, the PWA manifest icons, the push icon and `public/img/icons/favicon.ico` are not touched (spec, "Not changing").

The tick's three polygons, in the 512 × 512 icon's coordinates (traced from `public/img/icons/android-chrome-512x512.png`, 98.9 % pixel overlap). `AppLogo.vue` (Task 1) and `index.html` (Task 3) both use exactly these points:

```text
short left bar:  8,286 127.5,405.5 158,375 38.5,255.5
short upper bar: 218,254 248.5,284.5 384,149 353.5,118.5
long tick:       129,286 248,405 504.5,148.5 474,118 248,344 159.5,255.5
```

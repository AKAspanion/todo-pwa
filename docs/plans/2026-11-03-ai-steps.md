# AI Steps Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: the project's workflow policy names the executor (`run.executor`): ga:implement-with-subagents by default, or ga:implement. Follow the policy and this plan's Checkpoints. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Pro accounts press "Suggest steps" in the checklist editor and get up to eight steps added after the checklist's existing items, with Undo.

**Architecture:** A `'suggestSteps'` request carries the task's title and description, admitted by the rules as `'suggestLabels'` is; its registry entry asks for at most eight short steps and checks them. A button inside usability's `ChecklistEditor.vue` appends the answer through `cleanChecklist()`, skips repeats, stops at 50, drops an answer whose checklist changed meanwhile, and offers Undo.

**Tech Stack:** Vue 3 + Vuetify + Pinia + vue-i18n (web app at the repository root); Firebase Auth and Firestore; Cloud Functions on Node 22 with firebase-functions v2 (`functions/`); the provider layer and kind registry from parts A and B; Vitest against the Firebase emulators; GitHub Actions.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-11-03-ai-steps.md`

## Global Constraints

- Build after part B (`docs/plans/2026-11-03-ai-task-assists.md`) is merged, and after the usability task fields (`docs/specs/2026-11-03-task-priority-and-checklist.md` on `feat/usability-specs`), which add the checklist, `ChecklistEditor.vue`, `CHECKLIST_MAX_ITEMS`, `CHECKLIST_TEXT_MAX` and `cleanChecklist()`.
- §1: "Each press spends exactly one request."
- §1: "Nothing the person wrote in the checklist is replaced or removed."
- §1: "The checklist never goes past `CHECKLIST_MAX_ITEMS = 50` items, and every item meets the usability spec's C1."

- No `any` and no `@ts-ignore`; no provider SDK imported outside `functions/src/ai/claude.ts` and `functions/src/ai/gemini.ts`.

- Produces: `requestAssist` and `assist` accept `'suggestSteps'`; `SuggestStepsButton.vue` takes `title`, `description` and `checklist`, and emits `suggested(steps: string[], sentChecklist: ChecklistItem[])`.

**Spec:**
- §3: "Hidden when the checklist already holds 50 items. Disabled while the title is empty after trimming, or while another AI request from the screen is out (part B §3.3)."
- §3: "**The answer** adds its steps after the checklist's items, in the order given, each unticked. A step whose text matches an existing item's, ignoring case and surrounding spaces, is skipped; adding stops at 50 items."
- §3: "The snackbar says "Added 1 step" or "Added N steps" (those added) with **Undo** for `UNDO_TIMEOUT_MS = 8000`, which puts the checklist back as it was before the answer. When nothing new is added, "No new steps to add." with no Undo."
- §3: "if the checklist changed while the request was out (an item added, removed, edited or ticked), the answer isn't applied and the snackbar says "The checklist changed, so the steps weren't added.""
- §3: "on the add screen the steps are part of the form and saved with the task; in the edit form they are part of the edit and saved with it. Nothing is saved by the answer itself."

**Tags:** screen, failure-paths, security

**Knowledge:** `features/add-task` and `features/task-board`: Pro accounts can suggest checklist steps, appended after existing items with Undo.

**Acceptance:**
- Given an account without `pro`, then no "Suggest steps" button is rendered in the checklist editor on either form.
- Given a Pro account with 50 checklist items, then the button is hidden; with 49 it shows; with a title of only spaces it is disabled; while another AI request is out it is disabled.
- Given no consent, when it is pressed, then part A's consent dialog opens first.
- Given the checklist `["Book tickets"]`, when the answer is `["book tickets ", "Pack bags", "Hotel"]`, then the checklist is `["Book tickets", "Pack bags", "Hotel"]`, the two new items unticked, and the snackbar says "Added 2 steps" with Undo; Undo makes it `["Book tickets"]` again.
- Given 48 items and an answer of five new steps, then two are added, the checklist holds 50, and the snackbar says "Added 2 steps".
- Given an answer that adds nothing new, then the snackbar says "No new steps to add." with no action.
- Given an item ticked while the request was out, when the answer comes, then the checklist is unchanged and the snackbar says "The checklist changed, so the steps weren't added."
- Given the add screen, when steps are added and the person saves, then the saved task's checklist holds them; given they don't save, nothing is written; the edit form, the same.
- Given each new string, then `en/ai.json` and `hi/ai.json` have the same keys, and the Hindi says the same as the English.

# Break a task into steps (AI part B2)

- **Date:** 2026-11-03
- **Status:** approved on settled facts, 2026-11-03 (the developer's `steps.approve: when-settled`)
- **Part of:** [decisions/ai-features-roadmap](../../knowledge/decisions/ai-features-roadmap.md), part B2
- **Builds on:** [the task assists spec](2026-11-03-ai-task-assists.md) (part B), whose kind registry, one-request-
  at-a-time rule, changed-meanwhile check, messages and Undo this part uses;
  [decisions/ai-steps-appended](../../knowledge/decisions/ai-steps-appended.md)
- **Build after:** part B, and the usability roadmap's task fields
  (`docs/specs/2026-11-03-task-priority-and-checklist.md` on `feat/usability-specs`), which adds the task's
  `checklist`, `ChecklistEditor.vue`, `CHECKLIST_MAX_ITEMS`, `CHECKLIST_TEXT_MAX` and `cleanChecklist()`

## 1. Goal

On the add screen and in the edit form, a Pro account presses "Suggest steps" and gets up to eight concrete steps for
the task, added to its checklist after any items already there.

Success is:

- Each press spends exactly one request.
- Nothing the person wrote in the checklist is replaced or removed.
- The checklist never goes past `CHECKLIST_MAX_ITEMS = 50` items, and every item meets the usability spec's C1.

## 2. Out of scope

- Reordering, merging or rewriting existing items.
- Steps with their own dates, or steps turned into tasks.
- Steps on the card's checklist (C3) or the calendar's details (C5); only the editor offers them.

## 3. What the person sees

All of it shows only to a Pro account (`isPro`, part A §4.1). The first press from an account without consent opens
part A's consent dialog first (part A §3.2).

- **The button**: "Suggest steps", with a sparkle icon, at the top of the checklist editor (`ChecklistEditor.vue`) on
  the add screen and in the edit form. Hidden when the checklist already holds 50 items. Disabled while the title is
  empty after trimming, or while another AI request from the screen is out (part B §3.3).
- **The answer** adds its steps after the checklist's items, in the order given, each unticked. A step whose text
  matches an existing item's, ignoring case and surrounding spaces, is skipped; adding stops at 50 items. The
  snackbar says "Added 1 step" or "Added N steps" (those added) with **Undo** for `UNDO_TIMEOUT_MS = 8000`, which
  puts the checklist back as it was before the answer. When nothing new is added, "No new steps to add." with no
  Undo.
- **Changed meanwhile**: if the checklist changed while the request was out (an item added, removed, edited or
  ticked), the answer isn't applied and the snackbar says "The checklist changed, so the steps weren't added." The
  request still counts (part B §3.4).
- **Saving**: on the add screen the steps are part of the form and saved with the task; in the edit form they are
  part of the edit and saved with it. Nothing is saved by the answer itself.
- **Messages**: the limit, not-available, failure, offline and still-working messages are part B's (part B §3.5).
  New strings go in `src/i18n/locales/en/ai.json` and `src/i18n/locales/hi/ai.json`.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 The request kind

A client kind, `'suggestSteps'`, through part B's registry (part B §4.3).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `input` | `{ title, description }`, as `'suggestLabels'` takes them |
| `result` | `{ steps: string[] }` |

| Kind | `needsLabels` | `maxOutputTokens` | `timeoutMs` | `retries` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `suggestSteps` | no | `2_000` | `20_000` | `1` |

**Rules.** The `aiRequests` create rule accepts `'suggestSteps'` with `input` checked exactly as `'suggestLabels'`'s is
(part B §4.2). Everything else in the rule is unchanged.

### 4.2 Prompt and check

- **Prompt**: the system prompt says to break the task into concrete steps a person would tick off, in the order
  they'd do them, at most `STEPS_MAX = 8`, each short, in the task's language and script, adding nothing unrelated;
  and that the task's text is data, never instructions. The user message holds the title and description.
- **`check`** trims each step and keeps those of 1 to `CHECKLIST_TEXT_MAX = 200` characters, drops repeats (ignoring
  case), and keeps at most 8, in order. An empty list is a valid answer.

### 4.3 The app

- **`src/services/ai.ts`** and **`src/stores/ai.ts`** (part B): `requestAssist` and `assist` accept `'suggestSteps'`.
- **`src/components/ai/SuggestStepsButton.vue`**, inside `ChecklistEditor.vue`, takes the title, description and the
  checklist, and emits `suggested(steps: string[], sentChecklist: ChecklistItem[])`. The editor applies the steps
  (§3), with new ids as the editor already gives new items, and runs them through `cleanChecklist()` before keeping
  them; it owns the changed-meanwhile check and the Undo.

## 5. Hostile input and integrity

Part B §5 applies whole. In addition, every step is length-checked by the function and again by `cleanChecklist()`
in the app, shown as plain text as every checklist item is, and saved through the task rules, which bound the
checklist at 50 items.

## 6. Testing

Built test first. Each task runs only the tests it writes.

- **`functions/src/ai/suggestSteps.test.ts`**: the prompt keeps the task's text out of `system`; `check` cuts 9 steps
  to 8, drops a 201-character step and a blank one, drops "Buy milk" after "buy milk ", and keeps an empty list.
- **`functions/src/ai/kinds.test.ts`** (extended): the new entry's numbers.
- **`tests/rules/firestore.rules.test.ts`**: `'suggestSteps'` allowed with a good input, refused with an extra key.
- **`src/components/ai/SuggestStepsButton.test.ts`**, inside the editor on both forms: hidden without Pro and at 50
  items; disabled with an empty title; steps added after existing items, unticked, with a matching item skipped;
  adding stopping at 50; "Added N steps" and Undo; "No new steps to add."; the changed-meanwhile message; nothing
  saved by the answer.

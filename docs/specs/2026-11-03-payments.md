# Payments: buying Pro (AI part G)

- **Date:** 2026-11-03
- **Status:** written, waiting for the developer's review
- **Part of:** [decisions/ai-features-roadmap](../../knowledge/decisions/ai-features-roadmap.md), part G
- **Builds on:** [the AI foundation spec](2026-11-03-ai-foundation.md) (part A), whose `pro` claim, request-document
  pattern and admin script this part extends;
  [decisions/payments-play-and-razorpay](../../knowledge/decisions/payments-play-and-razorpay.md),
  [decisions/payments-without-http](../../knowledge/decisions/payments-without-http.md)
- **Build after:** part A. Built in two halves, each with its own plan: **G1, the web** (§4.1–4.5, §4.7) and **G2,
  Play** (§4.6), G1 first.

## 1. Goal

An account without Pro can buy it: on the website through a Razorpay subscription, and in the Android app through
Google Play Billing. Pro then unlocks at once, stays while the subscription is paid, and goes when it ends.

Success is:

- Pro is granted only after the server has verified the payment with the provider itself; nothing the app reports is
  trusted on its own.
- No card, UPI or bank detail ever reaches the app's servers or database.
- Inside the Android app only Play Billing is offered; on the website only Razorpay.
- A cancelled subscription keeps Pro until the end of the paid period; an ended, halted or refunded one loses it within
  a day (Razorpay) or within minutes of Play's notice (Play).
- Pro granted by hand ([decisions/pro-granted-by-admin-script](../../knowledge/decisions/pro-granted-by-admin-script.md))
  is never taken away by payments.
- No function answers HTTP or callable requests.

## 2. Out of scope

- Prices, plans' names and trials in code: they are set in the Play Console and the Razorpay dashboard.
- Coupons, family sharing, gifting, upgrades between plans mid-period (Play handles its own plan changes; Razorpay
  plan changes are cancel and buy again), and paying for more than one account.
- Invoices and GST in the app: Razorpay and Play issue their receipts.
- India's alternative billing inside the Android app.
- Plans of different sizes for AI use: every Pro account has the same `AI_MONTHLY_LIMIT = 300`.

## 3. What the person sees

### 3.1 Get Pro

- On the profile screen, an account without Pro sees a "Get Pro" card: "AI help for your tasks: fill from text,
  suggestions, voice and photos, daily plans and more." with a "See plans" button to `/pro`. This replaces part A's
  rule that non-Pro accounts see nothing of Pro (part A §2).
- **`/pro`** (`src/views/ProView.vue`), for signed-in accounts without Pro; an account with Pro is sent to `/profile`.
  It lists the Pro features, then the plans the current place offers (§3.2, §3.3), each with its name and price as the
  provider reports it, and a button "Subscribe".
- While the plans load, a progress indicator; if they can't load, "Plans didn't load." with Try again.
- With no plan configured for the current place, "Pro isn't available here yet."

### 3.2 On the website

- The plans come from Razorpay (§4.3). "Subscribe" opens Razorpay Checkout for that plan.
- When Checkout reports success, the screen says "Confirming your payment…" until the server has verified it (§4.3),
  then "You're on Pro." and goes to `/profile`. Pro features appear at once (§4.2).
- If the person closes Checkout, nothing happens and the plans stay. If Checkout reports a failure, "The payment
  didn't go through. Nothing was charged." If the server can't verify it, "We couldn't confirm that payment. If you
  were charged, it will be refunded or your Pro will start within a day." (the daily check, §4.4, picks up a payment
  that was real.)
- Inside the Android app, the website's checkout is never shown, even if the page is opened there.

### 3.3 In the Android app

- The plans come from Play through the Digital Goods API (§4.6). "Subscribe" opens Play's purchase sheet.
- After Play reports the purchase, "Confirming your purchase…", then "You're on Pro." as on the web. If the server
  refuses the token, "We couldn't confirm that purchase. If you were charged, Google Play will refund it."
- In a browser without the Digital Goods API inside the app (an old Chrome), "Update Chrome to subscribe in the app."

### 3.4 Managing Pro

On the profile screen, an account with Pro sees a "Pro" card:

- **Razorpay**: "Renews on <date>" or, once cancelled, "Ends on <date>"; a "Cancel subscription" button, which asks
  "Cancel Pro? You'll keep it until <date>." with Cancel subscription and Keep. Cancelling takes effect at the end of
  the period (§4.3). A failed cancel: "Couldn't cancel. Try again."
- **Play**: "Renews on <date>" or "Ends on <date>", and "Manage in Google Play", opening Play's subscriptions page for
  the app's package.
- **Manual**: "Pro, given to this account." and nothing to manage.
- An account on Razorpay that opens the Android app sees "Manage on the website" instead of a cancel button, and the
  reverse for Play on the website.

### 3.5 Messages

All in English and Hindi, in `src/i18n/locales/en/pro.json` and `src/i18n/locales/hi/pro.json`.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 The entitlement

**`entitlements/{uid}`**, written only by functions (the Admin SDK); the owner may read it.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `source` | `'razorpay'`, `'play'` or `'manual'` |
| `status` | `'active'`, `'cancelled'` (paid up but not renewing) or `'expired'` |
| `planKey` | `'monthly'` or `'yearly'` for paid sources; `null` for manual |
| `expiresAt` | The end of the paid period, or `null` for manual |
| `ref` | Razorpay's subscription id, or the hash of Play's purchase token |
| `updatedAt` | When a function last wrote it |

- **`applyEntitlement(db, auth, uid, next)`** in `functions/src/payments/entitlement.ts` is the only code that
  writes it. In one step it writes the document and sets the `pro` claim: on when `status` is `'active'` or
  `'cancelled'` and `expiresAt` is in the future (or `null` for manual), off otherwise. It merges `pro` into the
  account's other claims, as part A's script does.
- **Manual wins.** When the current entitlement's `source` is `'manual'`, a payment for the same account never
  replaces it; the payment is still recorded (its `paymentRequests` answer says so) so nothing is lost.
- **Part A's admin script** (`functions/src/admin/pro.ts`) writes a manual entitlement through `applyEntitlement`
  when it grants, and marks it `'expired'` when it revokes.

### 4.2 The app picks up changes

The session store watches `entitlements/{uid}` while signed in. When it changes, the store refreshes the ID token
once (`getIdToken(true)`), so `isPro` (part A §4.1) follows a purchase or an expiry without signing out.

### 4.3 Razorpay (G1)

Requests travel as **`paymentRequests/{id}`** documents, answered by **`answerPaymentRequest`**, a Firestore-triggered
function, in the pattern of part A's `aiRequests` (claimed once in a transaction, answered on the same document,
expiring after a day by a TTL policy on `expireAt`). The owner may create them, with the shapes below, and read and
delete their own; nobody may update them.

| Kind | `input` | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| `listPlans` | `{}` | `{ plans: { key, name, amount, currency, period }[] }` |
| `startRazorpay` | `{ planKey: 'monthly' \| 'yearly' }` | `{ subscriptionId, keyId }` |
| `confirmRazorpay` | `{ paymentId, subscriptionId, signature }` | `{ status: 'active' \| 'manual' }` |
| `cancelRazorpay` | `{}` | `{ endsAt }` |

- **`listPlans`** fetches the plans named by the params `RAZORPAY_PLAN_MONTHLY` and `RAZORPAY_PLAN_YEARLY` (either may
  be empty) from Razorpay's API, and answers their names, amounts, currency and period. It never answers a plan that
  isn't configured.
- **`startRazorpay`** refuses an account that already has an active or cancelled paid entitlement, then creates a
  Razorpay subscription for the configured plan with `notes: { uid }`, and answers its id and the key id.
- **`confirmRazorpay`**:
  1. checks `signature` as Razorpay's Subscriptions documentation specifies, an HMAC-SHA256 with `RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET`
     over the payment and subscription ids, compared in constant time; the exact payload is confirmed against those docs
     when it is built;
  2. fetches the subscription from Razorpay's API and requires that it exists, its `notes.uid` is the requester's, its
     plan is a configured one, and its status is `active` or `authenticated`;
  3. applies the entitlement: `source: 'razorpay'`, `status: 'active'`, `planKey`, `expiresAt` from the
     subscription's current period end, `ref` the subscription id.
  Any failure is answered `error: 'unverified'` and changes nothing. Confirming the same subscription again changes
  nothing more.
- **`cancelRazorpay`** cancels the requester's Razorpay subscription at the end of the current cycle through Razorpay's
  API, and applies `status: 'cancelled'` with the same `expiresAt`.
- **Checkout** is Razorpay's own script, loaded only on `/pro` and only outside the Android app. The Content Security
  Policy in `firebase.json` adds Razorpay's checkout script, frame and API origins to `script-src`, `frame-src` and
  `connect-src`, and nothing else.

### 4.4 The daily check (G1)

**`checkSubscriptions`**, `onSchedule('every day 03:00')` in `Asia/Kolkata`:

- For every entitlement with `source: 'razorpay'` and `status` other than `'expired'`, fetches its subscription from
  Razorpay and applies what it says: `active` keeps it active with the new period end; `cancelled` or `completed`
  makes it `'cancelled'` until `expiresAt`; `halted` or `expired` makes it `'expired'`.
- For a subscription that Razorpay reports active with `notes.uid` but whose account has no entitlement (a confirm
  that never arrived), applies it as `confirmRazorpay` would.
- For every paid entitlement whose `expiresAt` has passed, applies `'expired'`.
- Handles `PAYMENT_CHECK_CONCURRENCY = 10` at a time; one account's failure never stops the others.

### 4.5 Rules and data (G1)

- `entitlements/{uid}`: **read** `isUser(uid)`; no client writes.
- `paymentRequests/{id}`: **create** by `isUser(request.resource.data.uid)` with exactly `uid`, `kind`, `input`,
  `status: 'pending'` and `createdAt == request.time`, `kind` one of the four, and `input` of that kind's shape (ids
  as strings of 1–100 characters, `planKey` one of the two); **read, delete** by the owner; never updated.
- `playTokens/{tokenHash}` (G2): no client access.
- `firestore.indexes.json`: `entitlements (source ASC, status ASC)`.

### 4.6 Play (G2)

- **The Android project** (`packages/android/source`) adds android-browser-helper's billing extension
  (`com.google.androidbrowserhelper:billing`), its delegation service and payment activity, as Google's TWA guide
  describes, checked against the current guide when it is built, and a new version code.
- **In the app**, `src/lib/playBilling.ts` detects `getDigitalGoodsService`, gets the service for
  `https://play.google.com/billing`, reads the details of `PLAY_SUBSCRIPTION_ID`'s base plans (the app's build-time
  config `VITE_PLAY_SUBSCRIPTION_ID`), and buys through `PaymentRequest` with the Play method. On success it creates a
  `paymentRequests` document `confirmPlay { productId, purchaseToken }`.
- **`confirmPlay`** (in `answerPaymentRequest`):
  1. refuses a `productId` that isn't `PLAY_SUBSCRIPTION_ID`;
  2. checks the token with the Android Publisher API (`purchases.subscriptionsv2.get` for the package `PLAY_PACKAGE_NAME`)
     using the function's service account, which the owner grants access in the Play Console; requires
     `SUBSCRIPTION_STATE_ACTIVE` (or in grace period);
  3. in one transaction, ties the token's hash to the requester in `playTokens/{tokenHash}`; a token already tied to
     another account is refused;
  4. acknowledges the purchase if it isn't yet;
  5. applies the entitlement: `source: 'play'`, `status: 'active'`, `planKey` from the base plan, `expiresAt` from the
     line item's expiry, `ref` the token's hash.
  Any failure answers `error: 'unverified'` and changes nothing.
- **`onPlayNotification`**, a Pub/Sub trigger on the topic named by `PLAY_RTDN_TOPIC` (`play-billing`), receives Play's
  real-time developer notifications, looks the token up in `playTokens`, fetches its current state from the Android
  Publisher API, and applies it: renewed keeps it active with the new expiry; cancelled makes it `'cancelled'`;
  expired, revoked or on hold makes it `'expired'`. A notification for an unknown token, or one that fails to parse,
  is logged by its type only and ignored.
- `checkSubscriptions` (§4.4) also expires Play entitlements whose `expiresAt` has passed, as a fallback.

### 4.7 The app (G1, and G2's `playBilling.ts`)

- `src/services/payments.ts`: the only code that touches `entitlements` and `paymentRequests`.
- `src/stores/payments.ts`, a setup store, reset with the other per-user stores.
- `src/views/ProView.vue` and its route (`/pro`, `requireAuth`, sent to `/profile` with Pro);
  `src/components/profile/ProCard.vue` on the profile screen.
- `src/lib/razorpayCheckout.ts`: loads Razorpay's script on demand and opens Checkout; never used inside the Android
  app (detected as the TWA, by the Digital Goods API or the TWA's referrer, as `src/lib/playBilling.ts` exposes).

## 5. Security and integrity

- **Nothing from the app is trusted.** Plans, amounts, statuses and periods come from Razorpay's or Google's API. A
  payment result from the app is only a pointer the function verifies (signature, then the provider's own record,
  then the account in the record).
- **No payment data is stored or seen.** Razorpay Checkout and Play take payment details; the app's database holds
  only ids, a token hash, statuses and dates.
- **One payment, one account.** A Razorpay subscription carries the uid in its notes and is checked against it; a Play
  token is tied to the first account that confirms it.
- **Repeats are harmless.** Every confirm and every check writes the same entitlement for the same facts.
- **Secrets**: `RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET` in Secret Manager, bound only to `answerPaymentRequest` and `checkSubscriptions`;
  `RAZORPAY_KEY_ID` is a param (Checkout needs it in the browser). Google's API is called with the functions' service
  account; no Google key is stored.
- **Logs** name the request id, kind, outcome and provider status; never a signature, token, payment id in full, or
  amount tied to an account.
- **Claims** are set only by `applyEntitlement` and the admin script, both server-side.
- **No new way in**: every new function is event- or schedule-triggered, and the entry-point test still fails on any
  HTTP or callable export.

## 6. Testing

Built test first. Each task runs only the tests it writes. Provider APIs are replaced by fakes in every test; no test
calls Razorpay or Google.

- **`functions/src/payments/entitlement.test.ts`**: each status and expiry sets the claim as §4.1 says; the account's
  other claims are kept; a manual entitlement isn't replaced by a payment.
- **`functions/src/payments/razorpay.test.ts`**: `listPlans` answers only configured plans; `startRazorpay` refuses an
  account already paid, and puts the uid in the notes; `confirmRazorpay` refuses a wrong signature, another account's
  subscription, an unconfigured plan and a `created` status, and applies a good one once; `cancelRazorpay` applies
  `'cancelled'` with the same expiry.
- **`functions/src/payments/check.test.ts`**: each Razorpay status maps as §4.4 says; a missed confirm is applied;
  passed expiries expire; manual is untouched; one failure leaves the rest checked.
- **`functions/src/payments/play.test.ts`** (G2): `confirmPlay` refuses another product, an inactive token, and a token
  tied to another account; acknowledges once; `onPlayNotification` maps renewed, cancelled, expired and revoked;
  ignores an unknown token and a malformed message.
- **The entry-point test**: the new exports have event, schedule or Pub/Sub triggers, and none is HTTP or callable.
- **`tests/rules/firestore.rules.test.ts`**: no client writes `entitlements` or `playTokens`; `paymentRequests` created
  only by the owner with each kind's shape, refused with an extra key, another uid, or a `planKey` of `'weekly'`.
- **`src/views/ProView.test.ts`** and **`src/components/profile/ProCard.test.ts`**: the Get Pro card only without Pro;
  `/pro` sending a Pro account to `/profile`; the plans with the provider's prices; each message in §3.2 and §3.3;
  Razorpay never offered when the Digital Goods API is present; the Pro card's text per source and status; the cancel
  question and its failure message.
- **`src/stores/session.test.ts`** (extended): a change to the entitlement refreshes the token once.
- **`tests/hosting/headers.test.ts`** (part C's, extended): the CSP allows Razorpay's origins in exactly the three
  directives.

## 7. Operations

The README gains a "Payments" section with the owner's one-time steps:

- Razorpay: business KYC; UPI Autopay switched on by Razorpay's support, in test and live mode; the monthly and yearly
  plans created; `RAZORPAY_KEY_ID`, `RAZORPAY_PLAN_MONTHLY` and `RAZORPAY_PLAN_YEARLY` set as params and
  `RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET` as a secret.
- Play: the subscription and its base plans (and any trial offer) created in the Play Console; the functions' service
  account given financial-data access; real-time developer notifications pointed at the Pub/Sub topic `play-billing`;
  `PLAY_PACKAGE_NAME` and `PLAY_SUBSCRIPTION_ID` set; the new app version released.
- Terms of service, a refund policy and the privacy policy updated; GST settled with the owner's accountant.
- The TTL policy on `paymentRequests.expireAt`.

## 8. What this binds later

- Account deletion also deletes `entitlements/{uid}`, the account's `paymentRequests` and its `playTokens`, and cancels
  an active Razorpay subscription first ([requirements/account-deletion](../../knowledge/requirements/account-deletion.md)).
- Any later paid feature reads Pro from the `pro` claim, never from `entitlements` directly, so manual grants and both
  providers keep working the same way.

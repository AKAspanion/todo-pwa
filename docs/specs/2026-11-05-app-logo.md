# The blue double tick, everywhere

## Goal

The app shows one logo, the blue double tick, wherever a logo appears: the browser tab, the installed icons and
the app's own first screens. Recorded as [decisions/app-logo](../../knowledge/decisions/app-logo.md).

## What the developer asked for

On 2026-11-05: "lets use this logo everywhere", with the tick from `public/img/icons/android-chrome-512x512.png`,
then three answers:

1. The logo is shown on the sign-in and sign-up cards, and on the loading screen in place of the spinner; not
   in the menu (1a). After seeing it in the browser, the developer asked for it inside the card, not above it.
2. The unused `src/assets/logo.png` (the Vue starter logo) and `src/assets/logo-white.svg` (an old white arrow)
   are deleted (2a).
3. In the app the tick is redrawn as an SVG, not the PNG (3a).

## Where the logo is today

Every PWA, Apple, Windows and Safari icon in `public/img/icons/`, the web push icon, the Android launcher, splash,
notification and store icons and the iOS wrapper's icons already show the tick. Only these differ:

| Where | Today |
|---|---|
| `public/favicon.ico`, the tab icon `index.html` links | a blue up arrow, from `dbddf1c` (2019) |
| `src/assets/logo.png`, `src/assets/logo-white.svg` | other marks, used nowhere |
| sign-in, sign-up and loading screens | no logo |

## Changes

- **L1 Favicon.** `public/favicon.ico` is the tick, drawn from the 512 px icon at 16, 32 and 48 px, on a
  transparent background. `index.html` keeps linking it.
- **L2 Leftovers.** `src/assets/logo.png` and `src/assets/logo-white.svg` are deleted. Nothing imports them.
- **L3 `AppLogo`.** `src/components/layout/AppLogo.vue` draws the tick as an inline SVG: three polygons traced
  from the 512 px icon (98.9 % pixel overlap), viewBox `0 104 512 308`, filled `#2196F3` in light and dark (the
  icons' own blue, see the decision for why not the light primary). It takes a `size` prop, the rendered width in
  px, default `72`. It is decorative wherever it is used (a heading names the screen), so the SVG is
  `aria-hidden="true"` and has no title.
- **L4 Sign-in and sign-up.** `src/views/SignInView.vue` and `src/views/SignUpView.vue` show `AppLogo`, centred,
  inside the card at its top, above the title, at `72` px wide. Nothing else on either screen moves or changes.
- **L5 Loading screen.** `index.html` shows the same SVG, inlined (Vue isn't loaded yet), `96` px wide, centred on
  the `#fafafa` background, in place of the spinner's ring. It pulses gently (opacity) while it waits; under
  `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` it holds still. It hides exactly as today, once `#app` has content.

## Not changing

The native Android and iOS icons, the PWA manifest icons, the push icon and `public/img/icons/favicon.ico`
already show the tick. The menu and the app bar get no logo.

## Testing

- `src/components/layout/AppLogo.test.ts`: it renders one SVG, hidden from assistive technology, at the width
  given (and `72` by default), filled `#2196F3`.
- The visual references of the sign-in and sign-up screens under `design/modernize/auth/` are captures of the old app,
  which had no logo; those two comparisons will show the logo as a difference until the references are refreshed.
  e2e runs only when the developer asks.
